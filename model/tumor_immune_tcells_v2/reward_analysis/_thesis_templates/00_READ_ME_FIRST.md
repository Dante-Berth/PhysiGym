# Thesis Templates: Copy-Paste Ready

These files contain copy-paste templates for your PhD thesis chapters.

---

## 📝 How to Use

1. **Open the template file** that matches your thesis section
2. **Copy** the template text
3. **Paste** into your thesis
4. **Customize** with your specific details (numbers, framing)
5. **Insert figures** from `../` _figures/` at the marked locations

---

## ✅ How to Customize

### Keep (don't change):
- Core arguments and evidence
- Figure/table references
- Data numbers (verified from search_results.csv)

### Customize (adapt to your style):
- Pronoun choice ("we" vs "I")
- Specific citations to your own work
- Section numbering (if different than 3.2 or 4.1)
- Brief motivating sentences (to match your voice)

### Replace with your data:
- Marked with `[YOUR_VALUE]` (none—all data already filled in)
- All numbers pre-filled from your search
